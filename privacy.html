Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-06-01  ·  Last updated: 2025-06-01

Who We Are

AmazonOps TikTok Publisher is a desktop application that helps Amazon e-commerce operators schedule and publish video content to their authorized TikTok business accounts. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how we protect it.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes — ever.

Data We Access

When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0, we request the following scopes:

Scope Purpose Stored?
user.info.basic Display your TikTok username and avatar in the app interface No — read at login only
video.upload Upload video files to TikTok as drafts for your review No
video.publish Publish video content to your TikTok profile when you confirm No

OAuth access tokens are stored locally on your device in an encrypted configuration file. They are never transmitted to our servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your TikTok password or login credentials
  • Your followers, messages, or private TikTok data
  • Your Amazon account credentials or order data
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or usage analytics
  • Any personal information beyond what TikTok's OAuth flow provides

How We Use Your Data

Data accessed through the TikTok API is used solely to perform the actions you explicitly request within the Application:

  • Displaying your connected account name so you can confirm the correct account
  • Uploading and publishing video content on your behalf when you click "Publish"
  • Maintaining your authenticated session so you don't need to re-login each use

No data is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the above.

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on the device running the Application. We operate no cloud database or user account system. Your OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and are only decrypted in memory during active API calls.

We implement the following protections:

  • OAuth 2.0 with PKCE for secure token exchange
  • HTTPS-only communication with TikTok's API endpoints
  • Tokens are scoped to the minimum permissions necessary
  • No logging of token values or API responses to disk

Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained on your local device until you explicitly disconnect your TikTok account within the Application, or until TikTok invalidates the token (e.g., password change, permission revocation). Disconnecting your account immediately deletes the stored token from your device.

Third-Party Services

The Application communicates exclusively with:

  • TikTok for Developers API — for authentication and content publishing. Governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Your Rights

You can exercise the following at any time:

  • Revoke access — Disconnect your TikTok account from within the Application at any time
  • Revoke via TikTok — Visit TikTok's app permissions settings to revoke access directly
  • Delete local data — Uninstalling the Application removes all locally stored tokens and configuration

Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for business operators and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
