Video Publisher Tool Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

Video Publisher Tool ("the App") is a desktop application that allows TikTok creators to manage, schedule, and publish video content to their authorized TikTok accounts via the TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Information We Collect

The App collects only the minimum information necessary to provide its services:

  • TikTok Access Tokens: OAuth tokens obtained through TikTok's official authorization flow, stored locally on the user's device.
  • Basic Profile Information: Display name and avatar, obtained via the user.info.basic scope, used solely to identify connected accounts within the App.
  • Video Content: Video files selected by the user for upload, processed locally and transmitted directly to TikTok's servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Access tokens are used exclusively to authenticate API requests to TikTok on behalf of the authorized user.
  • Profile information is displayed within the App to help users identify their connected accounts.
  • Video files are uploaded to TikTok servers using the video.publish and video.upload scopes as directed by the user.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the user's device. We do not operate any servers or databases. No user data is transmitted to any third party other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any user data to third parties. The only data transmission occurs between the user's device and TikTok's official API servers.

6. Data Retention

Users may delete their locally stored tokens at any time by removing the token files from their device. Revoking access through TikTok's app settings will also invalidate all tokens.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at: https://laoyanjing.github.io/privacy/
