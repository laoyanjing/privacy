2. Description of Service

Video Publisher Tool is a desktop application that enables TikTok creators to manage, schedule, and publish video content to their TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The App uses the following TikTok API scopes:

  • user.info.basic: To read your TikTok profile information (display name and avatar) for account identification within the App.
  • video.publish: To directly publish video content to your TikTok profile on your behalf.
  • video.upload: To upload video files to TikTok as drafts for further editing and publishing.
